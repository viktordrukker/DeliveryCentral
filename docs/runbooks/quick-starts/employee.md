# Employee — 1-page Quick Start

Welcome to DeliveryCentral. This is your 5-minute onboarding card.

## Logging in

1. Visit `https://<your-org-host>/login`
2. Use your work email + the password your admin sent you (or click Sign in with Microsoft if SSO is enabled).
3. After login you land on your **Employee Dashboard** at `/dashboard/employee`.

## What you'll do every week

### Log your hours (5 min, weekly)

1. Open **My Time** in the sidebar (or `/my-time`).
2. The current week is pre-selected. Click into a cell to enter hours per project per day.
3. Click **Submit week** when done. Your manager sees the submission within 30 seconds.
4. The dashboard shows your `Gap Days` (unsubmitted weeks). Aim for zero.

### Request leave (2 min, when needed)

1. **My Time** → **Leave Requests** tab → **+ New leave request**
2. Pick the dates + leave type (Annual / Sick / Other). Click Submit.
3. Your HR-approver gets notified. Track status under your dashboard "Pending" KPI.

### See your assignments

- **Employee Dashboard** → "Current Assignments" tile shows what you're working on now.
- "Future Assignments" tile shows what's coming up.
- Click any tile to drill down to the assignment detail.

### Report an issue (when needed)

- Click **Report an issue** from your dashboard (when JSM connector is configured — flag `integrationsJsm`).
- Pick a category, summarize the issue, click Submit. A ticket appears in your IT Service Desk.

## Settings you control

`/settings/account`:

- **Locale** — choose your preferred date / number format (default follows browser).
- **Timezone** — your week-start and date display follow this setting.
- **Dark mode** — UI theme.
- **Notification channels** — email + in-app + Teams (per channel admins enable).
- **Password** — change anytime.

## Need help?

- Click the **?** in the bottom-right (when Help Center flag is enabled).
- Contact your admin or IT Service Desk for access / permissions.
